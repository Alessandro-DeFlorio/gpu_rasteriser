# 0002 — License: MIT

Status: accepted (2026-11-23)

## Decision
MIT.

## Why
The owner preferred copyleft only if the choice could be reversed later. Relicensing is only partly
reversible:
- Copies already released under GPL stay GPL forever.
- Once outside contributors exist, relicensing needs their consent, or a CLA.
So we took the stated fallback: MIT.
